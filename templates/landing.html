Is Spinal really free to start?

Yes. Create an account and start talking. You don’t need a card to begin.

When is my camera on?

Only during a session you start, and only after your browser permission prompt. Close the session and the camera stops.

What does Spinal remember?

It keeps the things that matter between sessions, like your preferences and ongoing projects, so you don’t repeat yourself.

What do I need to run it?

A device with a camera and microphone and a modern browser. Screen share is optional.
