Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

At Spinal AI, your privacy is our priority. This policy explains how we handle your data.

1. Data Storage

We store the following information in our secure database (Supabase):

  • User Account: Email address and authentication details.
  • Chat History: Your questions and Spinal's responses, solely to provide you with a continuous conversation experience.
  • User Profile: Your name (if you choose to share it).

2. Camera & Screen Sharing

  • 🛑 NOT RECORDED: We do not record, store, or upload any video footage from your camera or screen.
  • Frames are processed in real-time using Google Gemini API and are immediately discarded after generating a response.
  • No facial recognition data or biometric data is extracted or stored.

3. Microphone (Voice Input)

  • Audio is processed locally via your browser's Web Speech API.
  • Audio is not transmitted to our servers. It is converted to text directly in your browser.

4. Your Control

  • You can delete all your data anytime using the "Delete My Data" button in the dashboard sidebar.
  • You can revoke camera/microphone access anytime via your browser settings.

5. Third-Party Services

We use Google Gemini API for AI processing. Please refer to Google's privacy policy for details on how they handle data.
